# Humanizer: Remove AI Writing Patterns

Rewrite AI-sounding text so it reads naturally like a human writer, not a chatbot. Preserve factual meaning, arguments, and intent.

## Core Directives

1. **Eliminate Staging & Run-ups**:
   - Cut standalone throat-clearing ("Let's dive in", "Here's what you need to know", "Honestly?", "Look, ...").
   - Cut fake defenses against unspoken objections ("This isn't to say...", "Don't get me wrong...", "One might think...").

2. **Dismantle "Not X, but Y" Contrasts**:
   - Spot formulas like: *not just X, but Y*, *it's not X, it's Y*, *X rather than Y*.
   - Replace with direct positive assertions. (e.g. Instead of "It's not just a tool, it's an ecosystem", write "It provides an integrated ecosystem").

3. **Kill Dramatic Closers & Aphorisms**:
   - Remove pseudo-profound one-line paragraphs ("That is the real win.", "Let that sink in.", "Read that again.").
   - Replace generic sayings ("X is the currency of Y") with concrete, factual claims.

4. **Break Up Artificial Triads**:
   - Avoid forcing lists of exactly three items ("speed, scalability, and security") unless the domain strictly requires all three.

5. **Natural Rhythm & Punctuation**:
   - Vary sentence lengths organically (mix short and medium sentences).
   - Avoid over-reliance on em-dashes (`—`) in every paragraph.
   - Use plain, unpretentious vocabulary; avoid corporate/LLM buzzwords (*delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, beacon*).
